## Evidence and decisions

1. Vibe: user requests “clean modern facelift”, “very Direct To Consumer”, no cursive display text. Authored direction: precise, tactile, quietly cinematic. Brand assets come exclusively from the existing THREE website.
2. Journey: user requests full-span differentiated headers, compact sections, floating dropper interaction, product surfaces over imagery, reviews and individual store pages. Authored sequence: introduce GLP THREE; discover the range; understand the bottle; understand THREE science; read customer voices; answer practical questions; shop.
3. Energy: restrained opening film, quiet catalog, strongest spatial movement in the bottle reveal, then calm proof and a clear purchase path.
4. Feeling and memory: clarity, choice, curiosity, delight, confidence, reassurance, readiness. The dropper lifts as the visitor moves through the explanation.
5. Signature: “when you scroll down the dropped pulls slightly out of the bottle”. Build a code-driven split image with the real cap and bottle, plus a glass pipette illustration. This illustrates the packaging, not a scientific mechanism.
6. Range: premium minimal. No cursive, no oversized filler gaps, no aggressive sales badges, no distributor-first messaging.
7. World: distinct compact photographic scenes; native scrolling. No continuous worldflight, no scroll hijacking.
8. Assets: real white THREE logo, existing product photos, existing lifestyle/science photographs. Recent Higgsfield history checked; no relevant THREE generation found in the newest 90 entries. New generation is authorized only for the header, maximum total 500 credits.

## Design sentence

This page should feel precise and human because THREE brings complex nutrition into an everyday routine, expressed through real products, clear type, and the simple act of opening a bottle.

## Grammar

Compact product editorial: a persistent shopping navigation; an image-led product opening; a real catalog rail immediately below; one spatial product explanation; a full-bleed science scene; concise source-attributed proof; practical answers; an actual product choice. This custom grammar forbids long pinned arguments, distributor opportunity pitches in the main journey, blank scroll, fake reviews, and disconnected decorative 3D.

The provided Seed reference supplies light sans typography, pill controls, compact layouts, and frosted surfaces. Palette and identity come from THREE, not Seed. Nutrafol supplies easy-to-find product choices and practical detail pages. Alternate direction considered: centered white clinical typography with a symmetrical bottle. Chosen direction: asymmetric photography with a stable left reading column and a tactile product scene, which better serves the requested cinematic opening.
